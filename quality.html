MOLECULAR VACUUM BONDING

Why PVD Gold Coating is Revolutionary

Traditional gold-plated fashion jewellery relies on thin electroplating that wears off within weeks, exposing copper or brass that oxidizes and stains your skin green.

Mishme uses advanced Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD). Inside a high-vacuum chamber, vaporized 18K gold bonds atomically to medical-grade 316L stainless steel, creating a finish that is 10 times thicker and virtually scratch-impervious.
